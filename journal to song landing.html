Questions

Before you send your page

Is my journal entry private?

Yes. Your page is only used to write your lyrics. I never share it or post it without your permission.

Do I get a recorded song?

Choose Hear It Sung and your lyrics become a complete, produced song sung by my artist Alisha Inari, delivered as an audio file you can keep and play anytime.

What if my entry is messy or really long?

Messy is perfect. Send one entry up to about 500 words, roughly one handwritten page. If it's longer, I'll pull out the heart of it, or you can add the Long Entry option at checkout for full-length pages.

Can I give this as a gift?

Yes. Buy the spot, then have her send her own page, or send me a letter you wrote to someone you love.

Who owns the lyrics?

I write the lyrics, so I keep the copyright and songwriter credit. Every order includes a personal-use license: keep it, play it, share it and gift it. Want to release it on Spotify or perform it for pay? Add the Release License at checkout. Full details are in the Terms & Song License.
