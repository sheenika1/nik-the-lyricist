Questions

Before you send your story

Will I get it before Thanksgiving?

Yes, if you order by November 15 and send your family's story within 48 hours. Songs are delivered by November 24, so you have time to share it before the big day.

Can the song honor someone we've lost?

Absolutely. Tell me about them, a favorite dish, a saying or their seat at the table, and I'll write them in with love.

How many names and memories can I include?

Send as much as you like. A song fits about 8 to 12 names and a handful of memories best, and I'll choose the moments that sing.

Can we play it at dinner and share it with family?

Yes. Play it at gatherings, send it to relatives and post it on your personal social media. Releasing it on streaming services or using it commercially needs the Release License. Full details are in the Terms & Song License.

What if I'm ordering after November 15?

Email me before you order. If I still have space, Rush Delivery at checkout can get your song to you in time.

Can my song include explicit lyrics or cursing?

No. Every song I write is clean: no profanity and no explicit content. That keeps your song something you can proudly play for anyone, from the grandkids to Grandma.
